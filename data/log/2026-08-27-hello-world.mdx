In 1950 Alan Turing pointed the Manchester Mark 1 at the zeros of the Riemann zeta
function. Seventy-six years later, used datacenter GPUs sell on eBay for less than a
nice bicycle, and local AI models can read the literature, generate conjectures, write
GPU kernels, and check each other's work.

ALAN is the bet that a garage-scale machine, run continuously against **many parallel
avenues** on the Riemann Hypothesis, in tight human/AI collaboration, is now a
rational research instrument.

The design rules:

1. **Phased.** Each phase (~$8k seed → ~$20k scale → heavy) must earn the next with
   evidence, not vibes.
2. **Used parts.** eBay 3090s at ~$750/24 GB are the best $/VRAM in history.
3. **5 kW envelope.** Home solar + battery. Power-limit everything; move to an
   industrial site only if a phase truly demands it.
4. **Portfolio research.** Many avenues — operator searches, positivity criteria,
   large numerics, conjecture mining — promoted or killed on results.

This site is the machine's design document, lab notebook, and (soon) mission
control. Everything lives in the open.
